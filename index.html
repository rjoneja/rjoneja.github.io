    About Ordo

    Put things in the right order, fast. A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone, plus practice categories whenever you like.

    Made in London. No ads, no sign-up.

    Ordo Kids: a picture-first version for ages 6 to 8.

    Your data

    Your streak and scores are kept on this device. To count players and run the shared top scores, the game sends your daily score with a random ID that isn't linked to you. If you add your name to a board, everyone can see it. No cookies.
